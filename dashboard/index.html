HOW TO READ THE MODEL

Transparent by design

The prototype separates observed demand from hypothetical operating assumptions, then tests those assumptions against a fixed service definition. It supports comparison and stress testing, not automated staffing decisions.

01

Observed demand

Retained 2023 order-creation records are aggregated by recorded date and order-size segment. Missing dates remain gaps rather than assumed zero-demand days.

02

Scenario engine

Precomputed cases vary scheduled workers, overtime, temporary coverage, productivity, absence, and action lead time. All staffing and cost inputs are hypothetical.

03

Service measure

A case meets the target when at least 95% of units are completed within two operating days. Later-period validation is shown separately from the full replay.

04

Decision rule

Compare feasible policies on service, total modeled cost, backlog, and stress behavior. A passing case is a candidate for validation, not a prescribed headcount.
